What's working

Lift = a post's reach divided by its own account's normal (median of nearby posts), so 1.0× is an ordinary post for that account and 2.0× is double. Hit rate = share of posts at 1.5× or better. Faded rows have fewer than 4 posts: a hint, not a finding. Personal YouTube and replies are left out.

Up next on @1clawAI

Every queued post gets a predicted lift from how posts like it have done for us (topic, opening, length, hashtags, tags, link). Drafts under 0.85× get one rewrite, and the rewrite is kept only if it scores clearly higher and passes the writing check. Posts marked "repost" are proven winners from another platform.

Experiments

Each "test next" from the weekly briefing becomes a tracked test. Posts after the start date that did the thing are compared with the ones that didn't, on lift. A test ends after at least 6 posts on each side and 10 days, or after 28 days. A result of 1.2× or better counts as worked, and a test that worked with a wording change gets added to the rules the content generators follow.
